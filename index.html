Manga Teller

Manga Teller is a YouTube channel with podcast-style discussions of manga: two hosts going through a story chapter by chapter, with original commentary and original art.

Manga Teller Uploader is the channel owner's private tool for uploading these videos to the Manga Teller YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.
