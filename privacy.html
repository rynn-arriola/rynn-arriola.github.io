Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Manga Teller Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Manga Teller" to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, add captions, and manage playlists on the owner's own channel. The app does not access, collect, or store data about any other person.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never shared, sold, or sent to any third party. The app uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.

Sharing

The app does not share any data. Videos are uploaded only to the owner's YouTube channel, under YouTube's own terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Contact the owner through the Manga Teller YouTube channel.
